This Privacy Policy describes how FPS Football League 3D ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by Duffy, collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application. By using the Game, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate your identity and maintain your game session
  • Save your game progress and in-app purchase status
  • Serve advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Process and verify in-app purchases through RevenueCat and Google Play Billing
  • Improve the Game's performance and user experience

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Google AdMob: Device advertising ID and device information are shared with Google for the purpose of serving ads.
  • RevenueCat: Your anonymous user ID and purchase data are shared with RevenueCat for purchase verification.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the Game's services. Specifically:

  • Account data (user ID, email, profile): Retained until you request deletion
  • Purchase records: Retained for the lifetime of the account to ensure entitlements persist

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time.

To delete your account:

  • In-App: Open the Game → Main Menu → tap "🗑️ Delete Account" → Confirm deletion
  • By Email: Send a deletion request to luffyboa66@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"

Upon deletion, we will permanently remove your account data, game progress, custom uploads, and ad campaign data. This action cannot be undone.

8. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at luffyboa66@gmail.com.

9. Advertising

The Game displays advertisements through Google AdMob. These ads may be personalized based on your Google advertising preferences. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google Settings → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization".

You may purchase the "Remove Ads" subscription within the Game to disable Google AdMob advertisements.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information:

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • Database access is controlled through Row Level Security (RLS) policies
  • API keys and secrets are stored securely and never embedded in client-side code

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Continued use of the Game after any modifications to this Privacy Policy constitutes your acknowledgment of the modifications and your consent to abide by the updated policy.
